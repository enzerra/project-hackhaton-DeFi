## Overview

BOTFlow AI UI fuses **Architectural Cyber-Minimalism** with high-contrast DeFi precision. It delivers a sleek, glassmorphic dark dashboard with vibrant cyan-purple glowing accents, zero fluff, and instant clarity for batch token distributions.

## Colors

- **Primary (#080C14):** Obsidian background for maximum contrast.
- **Surface (#182236):** Translucent glass panels with subtle border highlights.
- **Accent Cyan (#00F2FE):** Primary interactive highlight and status indicators.
- **Accent Purple (#7928CA):** Secondary gradient glow for AI and execution states.
- **Text Heading (#F8FAFC):** Crisp pure white for typography hierarchy.

## Typography

- **Display (Outfit):** Modern geometric sans-serif for headings, stats, and badges.
- **Body (Inter):** Clean, highly legible sans-serif for inputs, table data, and descriptions.

## Elevation & Depth

Glassmorphism backdrop filters (`blur(16px)`), combined with multi-layered subtle shadows and glowing ambient backdrop filters.
