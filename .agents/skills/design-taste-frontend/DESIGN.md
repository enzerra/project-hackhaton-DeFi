## 2. Anti-Slop Design Directives (Strict Enforcement)

1. **Section Background Distinction**:
   - Each section MUST span full-width with a distinctly contrasting background color and sharp border separator (`border-y border-slate-200` / `border-slate-300`).
   - Section 1: Hero & 3D Mesh (`#FFFFFF` Pure White)
   - Section 2: Performance Comparison (`#F1F5F9` Cool Slate)
   - Section 3: Web3 Use Cases (`#FFFFFF` Pure White)
   - Section 4: 3-Step Workflow (`#F8FAFC` Soft Indigo)
   - Section 5: FAQ Accordions (`#E2E8F0` Warm Slate)
   - Section 6: CTA Final Banner (`#09090B` Obsidian Black)

2. **Typography & CTA Rules**:
   - Headlines: Max 2 lines on desktop (`text-4xl md:text-6xl font-extrabold tracking-tight`).
   - CTA Buttons: **No Multi-Line Text Wrapping**. All button labels must fit on 1 single line.
   - Contrast: 100% WCAG AA compliant (white text on black/dark background, dark text on white button).

3. **Interactivity & 3D Mesh**:
   - Interactive WebGL 3D Canvas node visualizer responding to mouse movement.
   - Interactive Live Test Simulator with step-by-step transaction state progress.
   - Interactive Gas Savings Calculator slider.
